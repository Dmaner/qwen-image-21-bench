# Provenance and third-party sources

The C1 experiment extends the A0 baseline by [luca-888](https://github.com/luca-888/qwen-image-21-bench), fixed at [`cf99e73c8fa1603718c47405730d94c9fbfe6e16`](https://github.com/luca-888/qwen-image-21-bench/tree/cf99e73c8fa1603718c47405730d94c9fbfe6e16).

- [A0_REVIEW.md](A0_REVIEW.md) separately reports Dmaner's 2026-10-10 BF16 reproduction, following A0's section structure with independently written text. Its two displayed outputs reuse the existing Phase 2 gallery files, verified byte-identical to both timing cohorts; the [publication audit](data/a0-cross/publication_evidence.json) preserves their original provenance and records the four unchanged profile-summary copies.
- `reproduce/scripts/c1_offline.py` and `c1_serving.py` adapt A0's `bench/a0_offline.py` and `bench/a0_serving.py`. C1 adds frozen component-quantization configurations, configuration hashes, isolated worker auditing, batched quality-case execution, and the 10-measurement protocol. It preserves the A0 prompts and separates initialization/diagnostics from headline timing. The current harness restricts execution to the four reported configurations; its timing and generation protocol is unchanged.
- `reproduce/reference/parse_diagnostics.py` follows A0's `modal_app.py` log-accounting conventions. The profiler export adapter and worker audit hooks instrument the fixed Omni source without changing model inference files; their diagnostics are separate from performance/quality runs.
- The bear reference image comes from A0's `inputs/qwen_bear.png`; its SHA256 is `69ac975d5408ebb8de85b1126ad5a37372621e416c4f1a9037cd2ba19b712817`. Input provenance does not give the image a new software license. Generated comparison images are identified by configuration/case and paired with their actual measured records.
- Model execution uses [vLLM-Omni `3dc35694`](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm-omni/tree/3dc35694b3d458fc1461a8fe71832ac12e8c8cea), vLLM0.31.0/PyTorch2.13.0+cu130, and `Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1@d26bb61231c349cf6b7896fa83353113880e1ba3`. Model weights and a full upstream repository copy are not included here; their own terms apply.
- [Supplementary LPIPS scores](data/phase2/quality/lpips/lpips_metrics.json) were computed on 2026-10-13 from the hash-verified original images using [LPIPS](https://github.com/richzhang/PerceptualSimilarity). The new [scoring script](reproduce/analysis/add_lpips.py) and results are separate from the original inference scripts and release ZIP; scoring-model weights are not included.
- The locally retained kernel-attribution evidence includes the installed vLLM `input_quant_fp8.py` and two Python kernels generated by PyTorch Inductor/Triton during this run. They remain third-party/generated dependency-source evidence. Their original headers, recorded source paths and SHA256 manifest are retained locally; public summaries report the attribution, while the full dependency-source payloads are omitted; they are not claimed as newly authored C1 kernels or placed under a new blanket license.

On 2026-10-11, the A0 repository metadata reported no license and its complete pinned tree contained no LICENSE/COPYING/NOTICE file. **No additional license or permission is asserted for A0-derived code or other third-party material.** Attribution and public availability do not establish a new license; third-party rights and applicable upstream terms remain with their owners. This repository does not apply a blanket MIT/Apache license to those materials.
